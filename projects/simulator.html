It runs entirely in the browser, with nothing to install.

Why I built it

Phase‑field simulations in COMSOL are powerful but slow and hard to share. I wanted a lightweight way to show the core idea of my research, that geometry and wettability can steer droplets, to people who don’t run CFD for a living.

How it works

Users adjust inputs and watch the flow respond in real time. Under the hood, the simulator uses simplified physics drawn from the same principles as my dissertation models: capillary pressure, wettability, and viscous flow in confined channels.

What I learned

Building an interactive tool forced me to decide which physics really matters to the story and which can be simplified, the same judgment a good process model needs.
